MULTIBOT2 v3.2.0
================

Architecture
- Automatic strategy discovery under strategies/.
- Stable Strategy / StrategyManifest / Signal contracts.
- Shared signal -> freshness -> duplicate -> risk -> trade -> persistence -> Telegram lifecycle.
- Canonical AssetConfig-owned market, currency and Sweep timeframe metadata.
- Generic registry-driven backtesting.

Built-in strategies

- Maximum two sends per signal identity.
- Supabase authoritative + SQLite fallback.
