MULTIBOT2 v3.3.0
================

Architecture
- Automatic strategy discovery under strategies/.
- Stable Strategy / StrategyManifest / Signal contracts.
- Shared signal -> freshness -> duplicate -> risk -> trade -> persistence -> Telegram lifecycle.
- Strategy-owned timeframes and data requests.
- Generic registry-driven backtesting.

Built-in strategies
- Trend Pulse 1.0.0 (adaptive_trend): 2 assets, 1D.
- Engulf 66 SMA 1.0.2 (engulfing_66_sma): 25 assets, 1h.
- Sweep 4H 2.1.0 (sweep_v2): 25 assets, 1h/4h canonical sweep schedules.

Locked rules
- Paper only, Yahoo only, 25 assets, ₹100,000, ₹2,000 risk, 1x leverage.
- Freshness exactly 1 hour.
- Maximum two sends per signal identity.
- Supabase authoritative + SQLite fallback.

Research
- 11 common backtest metrics.
- Transparent 0-100 rating with category breakdown.
- Strategy version + parameter snapshots.
